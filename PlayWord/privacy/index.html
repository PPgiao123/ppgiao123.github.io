1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the English vocabulary learning app and solo word game PlayWord (Android package ID: com.ppgiao123.playword and Windows desktop edition), developed and published by PPgiao123. It explains how v1.0.0 handles user and device information.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

PlayWord v1.0.0 is designed as an offline-first learning tool. It does not require account registration or sign-in, and the developer does not operate game servers or collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal information, usage telemetry, crash logs, contacts, photos, microphone audio, camera input, precise or approximate location, device identifiers, or Android Advertising IDs.

3. Local On-Device Storage & Backup Codes

To support offline study without an account, PlayWord stores your learning data strictly on your own device using local application storage:

  • Learning & review state: selected wordbook, FSRS-4.5 review schedules, mastered and review word lists, vocabulary assessment results and breakpoints, and solo duel statistics;
  • App preferences: interface language, reading voice accent, audio volume, screen shake toggle, haptic feedback toggle, and reading theme.

In Settings, you can optionally export or import a local compressed backup code (PW1:) via your device clipboard. Clipboard reads and writes occur only when you explicitly tap the export or restore buttons, and backup data never leaves your device unless you choose to copy and save it yourself.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

PlayWord v1.0.0 contains no advertisements and does not integrate any third-party advertising networks, analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or social trackers. Offline English pronunciation uses your device's built-in Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine locally. If a future version adds optional advertising, in-app purchases, or online features, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before that release is published.

5. Device Permissions

PlayWord requests only minimal permissions strictly needed for local features:

  • VIBRATE: optional tactile feedback on button taps (can be disabled anytime inside Settings);
  • android.intent.action.TTS_SERVICE query: checks your device's local Text-to-Speech voice availability for offline word pronunciation.

v1.0.0 does not request INTERNET, AD_ID, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or external media storage permissions.

6. Children's Privacy

PlayWord is designed for learners aged 13 and older (including high school, university, and adult exam preparation wordbooks) and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information to us (for example, by direct support email), please contact p981393625@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all study records and preferences remain solely on your device, you have full control over them at all times. You can erase all local PlayWord data by resetting progress inside Settings, clearing the app's storage in your operating system settings, or uninstalling PlayWord.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or privacy inquiries regarding PlayWord, please contact the developer at p981393625@gmail.com.
