ArrowGO

com.ppgiao123.arrowgo

ArrowGO is a minimalist arrow-extraction puzzle and local split-screen duel game developed by PPgiao123. Tap any arrow with a clear exit to slide it out along its path and clear the entire board.

Privacy Policy

Core Features

  • Campaign (45 Levels): Progressive puzzles featuring frozen arrows, key-lock chains, and shaped boards (Cross, Ring, Diamond, Heart).
  • Daily Challenge & Zen Random Mode: Deterministic daily seed puzzles plus relaxing random boards that charge free hints.
  • Local Versus Mode: Same-device two-player split-board battles and three AI difficulty tiers in BO3 / BO5 sets.
  • 5 Languages & Offline Play: English, Español, Français, العربية (RTL), and 简体中文—playable offline with no account required.
