ArrowGO

com.ppgiao123.arrowgo

ArrowGO is a minimalist logic arrow-extraction puzzle and local duel game developed by PPgiao123.

ArrowGO 是由 PPgiao123 开发的极简逻辑箭阵抽离解谜与本地对战手游。

Highlights / 核心玩法

  • Campaign (45 Levels): Progressive puzzles introducing frozen arrows, key-lock mechanisms, and shaped boards (Cross, Ring, Diamond, Heart).
  • Daily Challenge & Zen Random Mode: Deterministic daily seed puzzles and stress-free random boards with local progress saving.
  • Local Versus Mode: Same-device 2-player duel and 3 difficulty levels of local AI opponents (BO3 / BO5).
  • 5 Languages: English, 简体中文, Español, العربية (RTL), and Français.
