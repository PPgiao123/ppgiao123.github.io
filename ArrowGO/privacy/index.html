1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to the mobile and desktop game ArrowGO (Android package ID: com.ppgiao123.arrowgo), developed and published by PPgiao123. It explains how v1.10.2 handles user and device information.

2. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

ArrowGO does not require account registration or sign-in, and the developer does not operate game servers or directly collect, store, sell, or share names, email addresses, passwords, contacts, photos, microphone audio, camera input, or precise GPS location. On Android, optional rewarded advertising is served by Google AdMob over encrypted HTTPS connections as described in Section 4.

3. Local On-Device Storage

To preserve offline gameplay and rewards, ArrowGO stores game progress strictly locally on your device using standard application storage. This includes unlocked levels, star ratings, theme unlocks, daily challenge and random mode records, shared item inventory and coupon balance, remaining hearts or level timers, and user preferences (language, sound, vibration, and animation settings).

4. Third-Party Services, Advertising & Consent (Google AdMob & UMP)

In ArrowGO v1.10.2 for Android, players may choose to watch optional rewarded video ads (to claim coupon tickets or random campaign items) powered by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To load, serve, measure, and prevent fraud on ads, Google may automatically process the following categories of data over encrypted transport (HTTPS):

  • Device or other IDs: Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) and app-scoped or advertising-related device identifiers;
  • Approximate location: Coarse location inferred from your IP address;
  • App activity & diagnostics: Ad interaction events, crash logs, and SDK performance diagnostics used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

In the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, ArrowGO uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present a consent prompt before ad requests. You can review, update, or withdraw ad consent at any time inside the game via Settings → Local data & privacy → Ad privacy (when applicable in your region), or reset/delete your Advertising ID in Android system settings. Earlier offline releases (such as v1.8.3) and the Windows desktop build do not integrate advertising SDKs.

Learn more about how Google handles information from partner apps at Google Partner Sites Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Device Permissions

ArrowGO requests only minimal permissions required for optional rewarded ads and tactile feedback: INTERNET (for loading optional Google AdMob ads and UMP consent forms), com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (merged by Google Mobile Ads SDK for ad serving), and VIBRATE (for optional haptics enabled in Settings). ArrowGO never requests precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, or external media storage.

6. Children's Privacy

ArrowGO is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information to us (for example, via direct support email), please contact p981393625@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all game saves and preferences reside exclusively on your device, you have full control over them. You can delete all locally stored game data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android system settings or by uninstalling ArrowGO.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or privacy requests regarding ArrowGO, please contact the developer at p981393625@gmail.com.
